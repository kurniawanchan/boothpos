The **Sales** page (menu key `sales`) lists completed sales from the POS and from paid restaurant tabs. Use it to answer "what did we sell, to whom, paid how", to finish a part-paid sale, to verify transfer and QRIS payments, and to export. Pre-orders no longer exist and are not listed.

## Before you start

- Your role needs the `sales` menu. A second view, **Tables**, also needs the `floor` menu.
- Everyone with `sales` sees every transaction, not only their own.
- Cost and margin appear only for users who also have the `reports` menu.
- Dates are business dates (WIB), see [Business day](/concepts/business-day). The page opens on **today**.
- In DEMO mode you see only DEMO sales, in LIVE only LIVE sales, see [DEMO and LIVE mode](/concepts/demo-and-live-mode).

## What the list shows

At the top, four tiles: **Transactions**, **Units sold**, **Gross sales**, **Net sales**, followed by a revenue breakdown (net, order-level discount, tax and gross). Figures come from the server for the date range you picked, not from the filters below.

The **Transaction list** has one row per sale.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Select | Tick to include the row in an export or bulk verification. |
| Transaction # | Opens the transaction detail. Struck through with a **Voided** pill, or a **Refunded** pill, when applicable. |
| Customer | Customer name (click for a quick card) or **Walk-in**. |
| Outlet | The home outlet(s) of the items sold. Click a name to search by it. |
| Time | When the sale was made. |
| Cashier | Who took the sale. |
| Terminal | Tablet terminal name. Shown only when terminals exist. |
| Sold at | Outlet where it was sold. Shown only with more than one outlet. |
| Item | Number of item lines with the top two items. |
| Payment | Payment methods, plus **Partially Paid** (with the balance), **Unpaid**, **Not verified** or **Rejected** pills. |
| Margin | Margin amount and percent. Only with the `reports` menu. |
| Total | Order total. |

Click a column header to sort. A summary line under the title shows how many rows are shown, units, amount, cash, non-cash, outstanding balance, margin and voided count, for the rows that match your filters. Voided rows are not counted.

## Filters

Filters apply instantly and are kept in the address bar, so a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared. **Reset** clears them.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Search | Transaction number, customer, outlet or cashier. |
| Date | **Today**, **Yesterday**, and **From date** / **To date**. Changing the dates reloads data from the server. |
| Cashiers, Terminals, Shifts, Outlets, Payments | Multi-select. The shift list is built from the transactions in range. |
| Payment status | Needs verification, Rejected, Verified. |
| Settlement | Partially Paid, Unpaid, Fully Paid. |
| Customer | Named customers, Walk-in only. |
| Minimum / Maximum total | Amount range. |
| Show voided transactions | Includes voided sales (shown dimmed). This also reloads from the server. |

When exactly one shift is selected, a **Shift summary** strip shows opening cash, cash sales, expected cash, and counted cash and difference if it is closed.

## Open a transaction

Click the transaction number or **View details**. The dialog shows the time, customer, cashier, notes, every item (photo, name, variant, SKU, outlet, category, quantity, price, line discount), the totals and the payments. A voided sale shows a red banner with the reason.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a payment to a part-paid sale">
    When a balance remains and the sale is not voided, **Add Payment** (or **Add another payment**) is available to everyone. Choose the method, amount (not above the balance), optional channel, reference and proof, then **Save payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify a transfer or QRIS payment">
    A non-cash payment starts as **Not verified**. Another person, not the one who recorded it, clicks **Mark verified** and confirms. This is final: there is no way to undo it. Owners and admins can verify any payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach or edit proof, reference and notes">
    **Add confirmation** or **Edit confirmation** is available to owners, admins and the person who recorded the payment. At least one of proof, reference or notes is required. A new photo replaces the proof; the old file stays on record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a payment (owner and admin)">
    **Delete payment** removes a payment record; paid and balance are recalculated. It is blocked for voided sales and for cash payments that belong to an already closed shift.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Void a transaction

**Void transaction** (red button, bottom left) appears for completed sales to users whose role has the `settings` menu (owner and admin by default). Enter a **Reason for voiding** (required, up to 255 characters) and confirm. Items go back into stock, and the sale stops counting in reports. This cannot be undone.

<Warning>
The server answers "Only owner/admin can void a transaction." (403) to users who are not allowed, and 409 if the sale was already voided, or is an open tab (use **Void tab** on the floor instead).
</Warning>

### Receipt

**View receipt** opens the same receipt as after checkout, with image and PDF download. See [POS](/operations/pos). It is always in Indonesian.

<Note>
Refunds and receipt reprints with approval exist on the server but have no button on the web Sales page. The **Refunded** pill only reflects a refund made elsewhere.
</Note>

## Bulk verification and export

- Tick rows, then **Mark verified (N)** to verify the pending non-cash payments of all ticked transactions. The result lists how many were verified and how many were skipped and why: recorded by you, already verified, voided, or rejected.
- **Export this view (N)** exports every row currently shown. When rows are ticked it becomes **Export selected (N)**. The file is `transaksi-penjualan.xlsx` and uses the date range and **Show voided** setting, with margin columns only for users with the `reports` menu.
- **Select all** ticks every row in the current view.

## Tables view

If you have the `floor` menu, the **Tables** toggle shows a live board of the restaurant floor instead of the list. It refreshes automatically (and pauses while the browser tab is hidden) and ignores the list filters.

- Four tiles: open orders, occupied tables with occupancy percent, tables open for more than 45 minutes, and open order value.
- Tiles are coloured: empty, occupied, partially paid, over 45 minutes, inactive. Click an occupied tile for the order detail panel, which shows the items with their kitchen status, the totals and payments. From there you can **Open in cashier screen**, or **View transaction** once the order is paid.
- With no tables, the board links to [Restaurant setup](/operations/restaurant-setup) if you can open it.

## Troubleshooting

| Message or symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No transactions match the filters. | Filters exclude everything. | **Reset**, or widen the dates. |
| Failed to load. | The report request failed. | Use the retry link. |
| The amount is higher than the remaining balance. You can pay at most X. (409) | Added payment exceeds the balance. | Lower the amount. |
| This transaction is already fully paid. | Nothing left to pay. | None. |
| Cash payments need an open cashier session. | You added a cash payment without an open shift. | Open a shift, see [Cashier session](/operations/shifts). |
| This transaction is closed (handed over, cancelled or voided)... | Payments cannot be added or removed. | None. |
| You recorded this payment, so someone else must verify it. (403) | Same person tried to verify. | Ask a colleague, owner or admin. |
| Cash payments are verified when they are recorded. | You tried to verify cash. | None needed. |
| This cash payment belongs to a cashier shift that is already closed... | Deleting a reconciled cash payment. | Not allowed. |
| We could not confirm the payment was saved (connection problem). | Network or server error during Add Payment. | Check the payment history before trying again, to avoid a duplicate. |
