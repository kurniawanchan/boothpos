Reports, the Dashboard, and the Sales page group money by **business date**, not by the raw clock time of the record. This keeps a sale rung up at 02:00 on the night shift on the right trading day.

## How the business date is decided

1. The sale's time is converted to the **store time zone**. The default is `Asia/Jakarta` (WIB).
2. The **business-day cut-off** is subtracted. The default is `00:00`, so the business day matches the calendar day.
3. The resulting calendar date is the business date.

With a cut-off of `04:00`, a sale at 02:00 on 12 March counts toward 11 March. A sale at 04:00 counts toward 12 March.

<Info>
  The business date is **stored on the record when it is created or completed**. Changing the time zone or cut-off later does not move past sales to different days.
</Info>

## Set the time zone and cut-off

Owners and admins set these on **Settings → General → Business day**.

| Setting | Default | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store time zone | `Asia/Jakarta` | Pick from the list. A zone already saved but not in the common list is kept in the dropdown. |
| Business-day cut-off | `00:00` | 24-hour `HH:MM`, from `00:00` to `23:59`. |
| Cash difference tolerance | `5000` | Zero or more. Used when a shift closes. See [Cashier shifts](/operations/shifts). |
| Device offline after (minutes) | `5` | Whole number from 1 to 1440. |

An invalid value shows an error under the field and nothing is saved. See [General settings](/settings/general) for the full screen.

## Where it matters

- **Reports and Dashboard** filter by a business-date **range**. A range includes the whole of each business day from its cut-off to the next cut-off. See [Reports](/insight/reports).
- **Sales** lists transactions by business date. See [Sales](/operations/sales).
- **Shifts** record the business date they opened on. See [Cashier shifts](/operations/shifts).

<Tip>
  If a late-night sale appears on an unexpected day, check the cut-off first. A cut-off of `00:00` splits the day at midnight local time.
</Tip>
