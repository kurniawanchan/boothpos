title: "Ring up your first sale"

This walk-through takes about five minutes. You need a role with the **POS** and **Cashier Session** menus, and at least one active product with stock.

<Warning>
  Check the badge in the top bar first. In **DEMO** mode the sale is practice data and will not appear in LIVE reports. See [DEMO and LIVE mode](/concepts/demo-and-live-mode).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a cashier shift">
    Go to **Cashier Session**. Pick the **Outlet** if you have more than one, type **Opening cash (Rp)**, and click **Open session**. Every sale is tied to an open shift, and **Pay** is blocked without one. See [Cashier shifts](/operations/shifts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the POS">
    Go to **POS**. The product grid follows the outlet of your open shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products">
    Click a product card. A product with several variants opens **Pick a variant**. Click the variant you want. Use the plus and minus buttons on the cart line to change the quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Pay**, choose **Cash**, **Transfer** or **QRIS**, and click **Confirm & save transaction**. For cash, enter the amount received and check the **Change**. For Transfer and QRIS, pick a payment channel. See [Payment channels](/settings/payment-channels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the receipt">
    The receipt opens when the sale is saved. Download it as an image or PDF if the customer asks, then click **Transaksi berikutnya** for the next customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the shift at the end of the day">
    Go back to **Cashier Session**, count the drawer, type **Physical cash counted (Rp)**, and click **Close session**. If the counted cash differs from the expected cash, a note is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check your work

<Check>
  The sale appears in **Sales** with its order number, and the shift's **Transactions** and **Total sales** tiles went up.
</Check>

## If something goes wrong

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Open a cashier session first in the Cashier Session screen. | Open a shift (step 1). |
| Insufficient stock for variant ... | Lower the quantity or add stock. See [Stock](/inventory/stock). |
| Item cannot be ordered: sold out (86), discontinued, or not sold at this outlet | Remove it from the cart. |

The full list is on [POS](/operations/pos).
