The **Pembayaran** (payment) screen settles an order on the tablet. You can pay with **Tunai** (cash), **EDC** (card machine) or **QRIS**, and you can combine them on one order. When the order is fully paid, the **Pembayaran selesai** (payment complete) screen shows the change, the struk status and, for counter orders, the queue number.

## Before you start

- Open an order that has at least one active line, then press the pay button on the order screen. If every line is cancelled, nothing happens.
- Your shift must be open. Without one, a dialog **Belum ada shift terbuka** (no open shift) says "Pembayaran hanya bisa diterima setelah shift terbuka. Order tetap tersimpan." Its **Buka shift** button takes you to shift opening. See [Shifts](/android/shifts).
- **Waiters cannot take payment.** For a waiter, the pay button prints a pre-bill instead (its label is **Cetak pre-bill**), and the order stays open for a cashier. The pre-bill is marked **BELUM LUNAS** (not yet paid). See [Printing](/android/printing).
- Payments always go to the server. Cash, EDC and QRIS are recorded only while the tablet can reach the server. See [Offline and sync](/android/offline-and-sync).
- For EDC and QRIS, an owner or admin must first set up the channels. See [Payment channels](/settings/payment-channels).

## Pay with cash

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the amount due">
    The left card shows **Sisa** (remaining) in large type, **Terbayar** (paid so far), and one row per payment already recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the amount">
    **Tunai** is selected by default. Tap a quick button, or type an amount on the keypad and press **Terima tunai** (receive cash).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over change">
    If you took more than the remaining amount, the done screen shows **Kembalian** (change). The server calculates it, not the tablet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Quick cash buttons

The first button is **Uang pas** (exact amount) and always equals the remaining amount. The others are labelled **Tunai** and round the remaining amount up to the next Rp 10.000, Rp 50.000 and Rp 100.000. Duplicates are dropped and at most four buttons show. For a remaining amount of Rp 13.200 you get Rp 13.200, Rp 20.000, Rp 50.000 and Rp 100.000. Tapping a quick button records the payment immediately, with no confirmation.

### Keypad

| Control | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Digit keys and **000** | Add digits. The amount is limited to 9 digits. Leading zeros are removed. |
| Backspace | Removes the last digit. |
| **Nominal lain** (other amount) | Shows what you typed. |
| **Terima tunai** | Disabled while the amount is 0. Sends the typed amount as cash received, then clears the field. |

## Pay with EDC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap EDC">
    The **EDC** tile opens the EDC sheet. It is disabled while the tablet is offline or while a QRIS payment is waiting for verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount on the machine">
    The sheet shows **Masukkan nominal ini di mesin EDC** and the remaining amount. The EDC amount is always the full remaining amount. You cannot change it here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the bank">
    The sheet loads the bank channels from the server. With one bank it is chosen for you. With several, choose one under **Bank mesin EDC** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the approval code">
    Under **Kode approval dari slip EDC**, type the code printed on the EDC slip. Letters are made uppercase, other characters are dropped, and the field stops at 12 characters. The card number is never recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Simpan pembayaran">
    The payment is saved. If it covers the remaining amount, the done screen opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### EDC rules

- The approval code must be 6 to 12 letters or digits. Otherwise the field shows "Kode approval 6-12 huruf atau angka."
- If the same code was used in the last 2 minutes, a dialog **Kode approval sudah dipakai?** asks you to continue only if this is a different transaction. **Lanjutkan** (continue) sends it again with your confirmation. The 2-minute window is the server default and can be changed in POS settings.
- With no bank channel on the server, the sheet says "Server belum punya kanal bank (bank_transfer). Pembayaran EDC tidak bisa dicatat dari sini." and nothing is recorded.

<Info>
There is no **Transfer bank** button on the payment screen. Only **Tunai**, **EDC** and **QRIS** can be started on the tablet. A bank transfer recorded elsewhere, for example on the web, still appears in the payment list and on the struk as **Transfer bank**.
</Info>

<Note>
In the payment list, an EDC payment is always labelled **EDC BCA**, whichever bank you picked. The bank you chose is stored on the payment, but the row label is fixed. On the printed struk the same payment reads **EDC**.
</Note>

## Pay with QRIS

QRIS on the tablet is a static QR code. The customer pays from their own app, and you record the payment as waiting for verification.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the QRIS tile">
    The tile shows the provider name from the server (for example the name of your QRIS provider), or **QRIS** if none is set. The sheet title is the provider followed by the account owner's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the QR code">
    The left side shows the QR image with the provider and account name. The right side shows the amount due and the instruction **Cek bukti transaksi di aplikasi QRIS, lalu catat kode verifikasi di menu Order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Bayar">
    **Bayar** records the full remaining amount as a QRIS payment with the status **Menunggu verifikasi** (waiting for verification). The struk is queued for printing automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify later">
    Check the transaction in your QRIS app, then verify it. See the next section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### QR image states

| What you see | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spinner | The image is downloading. | Wait. |
| **QR belum diatur** | The channel has no QR image. | Ask an admin to upload one in payment channels, or use **Ganti gambar QR** if you see it. |
| **QR tidak dapat dimuat** with **Muat ulang** | The image exists but the download failed. | Press **Muat ulang** (reload). |

**Ganti gambar QR** (replace QR image) appears only when the server says your user may manage the QR. It opens the photo picker. Only JPG and PNG are accepted. A wrong type shows "Gambar QR harus berformat JPG atau PNG." An unreadable file shows "Gambar tidak dapat dibaca." On success the toast reads "Gambar QR disimpan."

The QRIS channel is reloaded each time the sheet opens, so a QR uploaded on the web shows up without restarting the app.

### Verify a QRIS payment

Open the order from **Order > Terbuka**. An order with a waiting QRIS payment opens on the payment screen. The QRIS row shows **Menunggu verifikasi** and a **Verifikasi** button (visible only while online).

| Step or control | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Verifikasi** on the row | Opens **Verifikasi QRIS** with "Cek transaksi \{amount\} di aplikasi \{provider\}, lalu masukkan referensinya." |
| **Referensi transaksi** | Type the reference from your QRIS app. The **Verifikasi** button needs at least 4 characters. |
| **Verifikasi** | Marks the payment verified. The toast reads "QRIS terverifikasi." |
| **Tidak ditemukan** (not found) | Marks the payment as not found. The row shows **Gagal** and the toast reads "Pembayaran QRIS ditandai tidak ditemukan." |
| **Tutup** | Closes the sheet. |

Verifying or rejecting needs the QRIS verify permission. If you do not have it, a lock icon shows on **Verifikasi** and the **Perlu persetujuan** (approval needed) dialog asks a supervisor or outlet manager to enter their PIN. See [Approvals](/android/approvals).

<Warning>
A QRIS payment that is still waiting for verification blocks any other EDC or QRIS payment on the same order, and blocks closing the shift. The payment screen shows "QRIS menunggu verifikasi memblokir pembayaran non-tunai lain pada tab ini, dan penutupan shift." Cash is still allowed. An order with a waiting QRIS payment also cannot be refunded until it is verified.
</Warning>

## Split payments and overpayment

You can pay an order in several steps, with any mix of methods. Each step adds a row on the left card, and **Sisa** goes down. A toast reads "Sisa \{amount\}." until the order is fully paid.

| Method | Can exceed the remaining amount? | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tunai** | Yes. | The extra is change. The order closes and the done screen shows **Kembalian**. |
| **EDC** | No. The amount is the remaining amount. | The server refuses a non-cash total above the remaining amount ("Pembayaran non-tunai melebihi sisa tagihan."). |
| **QRIS** | No. The amount is the remaining amount. | Same rule. |

The note on the left card reads "Tunai memberi kembalian. EDC dan QRIS tidak boleh melebihi sisa tagihan." The server also checks that change never exceeds the cash handed over.

## Done screen

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Lunas** and the receipt number | The order is paid and has a struk number. |
| **Kembalian** or **Total dibayar** | Change when above zero, otherwise the total paid. |
| **Diterima** line | The amount received. For all-cash orders it ends with "tunai". |
| Struk status | **Struk tercetak**, **Struk belum tercetak**, **Struk belum dicetak** or **Struk gagal dicetak** with the reason, followed by the receipt number. |
| Sync status | **Tersinkron ke server** when online, otherwise "Tersimpan di perangkat · dikirim saat online". |
| **Order counter baru** or **Kembali ke meja** | Starts a new counter order, or returns to the tables. |
| **Lihat struk** | Opens the receipt sheet. |

### Queue number

Counter (takeaway) orders get a queue number such as `A-012`. It restarts from `A-001` each business day for the outlet. The done screen shows it in a large card. Table orders have no queue number.

**Panggil pelanggan** (call customer) speaks the number aloud through the tablet's text-to-speech. Letters are read as letters and digits one by one in Indonesian, so `A-012` is spoken as "A, nol, satu, dua". The toast "Dipanggil \{number\}" always appears. A second tap while it is still speaking is ignored, and if the tablet has no speech voice installed it stays silent. The number is also printed on the struk and the kitchen ticket.

### Receipt sheet

**Lihat struk** loads the receipt from the server and shows it on a paper-style card with the store name, address, receipt number, time, cashier, queue number, terminal name, lines, subtotal, discount, tax, **TOTAL**, each payment and **Kembali**. A reprinted copy shows **SALINAN n**. **Cetak struk** prints it. Printing rules are in [Printing](/android/printing).

## Who can do what

| Action | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Take cash, EDC or QRIS payments | A cashier-type user with an open shift. Waiters only print pre-bills. |
| Verify or reject QRIS | QRIS verify permission, or a supervisor PIN approval. |
| Replace the QR image | Server-side permission to manage the QR. |

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Koneksi ke server terputus. | The tablet cannot reach the server. | Press retry on the banner. EDC and QRIS are disabled until it reconnects. |
| EDC dan QRIS butuh server. Pembayaran tunai tetap bisa dicatat. | Offline banner on the payment screen. | See the note below. |
| Pembayaran ditolak: shift sudah ditutup. Buka shift untuk menerima pembayaran. | The shift was closed, for example from the web. | Open a shift, then pay again. |
| Ada pembayaran QRIS yang belum dicek. Selesaikan dulu sebelum menerima non-tunai lain. | A QRIS payment is waiting. | Verify or reject it first, or pay the rest in cash. |
| Kanal QRIS belum diatur di server. | No QRIS channel exists. | Add one in [Payment channels](/settings/payment-channels). |
| Pembayaran non-tunai melebihi sisa tagihan. | EDC or QRIS above the remaining amount. | Refresh the order and retry. |

<Note>
ASSUMPTION: The payment screen says cash "tetap bisa dicatat" while offline, but the app sends cash to the server like every other method and has no offline payment queue in the code I read. While offline a cash payment fails with the network error toast. Confirm the intended behavior with the product owner.
</Note>
