# Quickstart: verifying 058

1. Label: open the QRIS sheet; the button reads "Bayar" in full (US1).
2. Payment: take a QRIS payment of the full amount with the printer connected. Confirm the payment is recorded and one struk prints without another tap (US2). This also settles the R1 question: if the sale stays open, report it so the plan changes.
3. Verification: the sale still shows "Menunggu verifikasi" until verified (US3).
4. Printer off: take a payment with the printer disconnected; the struk waits in the queue and prints after reconnecting (FR-007).
5. Double tap: tap "Bayar" twice quickly; one payment and one struk (FR-005).
